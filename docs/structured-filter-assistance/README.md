# Structured filter designs: rejected after real inference

Neither candidate is shipped. The ordinary production FilterAssistanceEngine, parser and native helper remain at the merged baseline.

The generic condition-kind/value Boolean plan compiled and passed ordinary compiler tests but matched zero of twelve real-model semantic requests. The typed semantic enum design also compiled and passed ordinary checks, yet matched only three of twelve; it regressed original exact-project scope and confused planned dates, deadlines, completion and Boolean grouping. Its apparent success on a deadline case also had the wrong OR structure, so matching the current rows alone is insufficient to prove general semantics. Both exact candidate sources and reports are retained in rejected-designs. These are distinct representation changes, not reruns of the previous rejected prompt-only changes.

The retained product change is a stronger quality probe. The oracle preserves all five original cases and adds seven involving tomorrow, completed work, child-project/date/priority conjunctions, exclusion, mixed priorities, deadlines and planned-date comparisons. Each authored oracle is checked against a correct expression and a plausible wrong expression. The driver now requires twelve unique matching-ID observations, an actual accepted marker, successful terminal exit, unchanged source identities and no model unavailability. Ordinary runs still skip inference. The new candidates failing does not prove current production success on these extra cases; the current engine remains known to fail two of its original five and is not broadly accepted.

Only fixed synthetic descriptions were supplied. There were no task/filter writes, private task/catalog inputs, cloud inference, settings or permission changes, extra repair calls, installed-app replacements or normal gate changes. The same installed Mac38 and its pending readonly postlaunch check remain intact. Phone installation remains deferred. Full filter quality, native assistance and remaining Px remain open; do not retry either rejected schema unchanged.

The retained fixture additionally includes two deadline counterexample rows (open priority3 and completed priority1 on the same deadline). They distinguish a required open AND priority1 from the rejected candidate’s accidental open OR priority1 match. They leave the original five expected ID sets unchanged. Rejected reports describe the earlier nine-row fixture; no unchanged rejected design is rerun, and the twelve-case current-production probe is not claimed to pass.
