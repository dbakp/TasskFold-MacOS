- Preserve the original P0 scope. Full Todoist grammar, project hierarchy/archive/templates, external mirroring, goals/Karma, advanced teams, location/urgent alerts and wearables retain their original later-phase priority. Recognized optional email reminders are future provider integration, not active delivery.
- Ordinary iPhone/iPad acceptance uses Xcode Simulators as requested. Actual APNs/distribution and genuine system privacy have separate prerequisites; physical-device combinations are not prerequisites for already accepted ordinary app journeys.
